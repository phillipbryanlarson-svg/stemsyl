# Authors

**Phillip Bryan Larson** created Stemsyl. That includes:

- the writing system: its stems and ticks, and its sound-to-colour-and-position scheme
- the designs of its keyboards
- the direction of all the work in this repository

The apps, build tools, tests, figures and documentation were written with the help of **Claude**, an AI model made by Anthropic, working from his designs and instructions.

In the apps' licence headers, "the Stemsyl authors" means the people listed here. Anyone whose contribution is accepted into the project is added to this list.

The word list draws on the work of others. [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md) credits them.
